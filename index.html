How It Works

Booking, in four simple steps

From first message to prasad delivery — here is how ceremonies with Subhveda unfold.

  1. 01

    Message Us

    Share your concern or ceremony on WhatsApp, or call us directly. No forms, no waiting.

  2. 02

    Muhurat & Date

    We suggest an auspicious date and muhurat for your ceremony, and confirm it according to your convenience.

  3. 03

    Sankalp & Vidhi

    The ceremony is performed with your name and gotra in the sankalp — join on-site, or participate live on video call.

  4. 04

    Live Video & Prasad

    Watch the full ceremony live, and receive blessed prasad delivered to your home.
