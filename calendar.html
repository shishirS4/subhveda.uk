Major festivals of 2026

Vrat calendar 2026

Ekadashi, Purnima, Amavasya and Pradosh dates for your city.

Ekadashi is taken at sunrise and Purnima at sunset. Festival observance can follow pradosh or midnight rules and may differ by a day.

Recurring vrats

Somvar (Monday), Mangalvar (Tuesday) and Shanivar (Saturday) fasts; Sankashti Chaturthi on Krishna Chaturthi; Satyanarayan Vrat on Purnima.

Eclipses in 2026

Solar and lunar eclipses with the greatest phase in the selected city's local time.

Times follow the selected city's timezone. A lunar eclipse is visible wherever the Moon is above the horizon. Sutak is traditionally observed only for an eclipse visible at your location.

Gochar — planetary transits

Sidereal (nirayana) positions of the nine grahas, read with the selected panchang reference.

The Moon changes rashi about every two days; slower planets show their next ingress date.

Retrograde periods 2026

Apparent backward motion of the five star-planets during 2026.

Rahu and Ketu are always retrograde. Dates are sidereal and may differ by a day from Western ephemerides.

Indicative dates only. The final muhurat depends on your kundali, lagna and local tradition.
