Astrology Services (Jyotish)

Vedic Astrology, or Jyotish Shastra, is the ancient Indian science of celestial influence. Our astrological consultations are based on the precise calculations of planetary positions at the time of your birth, providing deep insights into your life's purpose, challenges, and opportunities.

What We Offer

  • Birth Chart (Kundli) Analysis: Comprehensive reading of your natal chart covering all 12 houses, planetary placements, and their influences on various life aspects.
  • Horoscope Matching (Kundli Milan): Traditional Ashtakoot matching for marriage compatibility, including detailed Gun Milan and Manglik analysis.
  • Annual Predictions (Varshphal): Year-ahead forecasts based on Tajik astrology to help you plan major decisions.
  • Muhurta Selection: Auspicious timing for weddings, griha pravesh, business launches, and other important events.
  • Remedial Consultation: Personalised gemstone recommendations, mantra prescriptions, and ritual suggestions based on your chart.
  • Compatibility Analysis: Relationship compatibility assessment for business partnerships and personal relationships.

Our Approach

We follow the Parashari system of astrology as the primary framework, supplemented with insights from Jaimini and Nadi traditions. Our readings combine classical text interpretation with practical, actionable guidance for modern life.

Traditional Methods

Planetary Worship

Nav Grah Pujan

The Nav Grahas (nine celestial bodies) profoundly influence every aspect of human life. Nav Grah Pujan is the sacred worship of these nine planetary deities to harmonise their energies, mitigate malefic effects, and invite positive cosmic influences.

The Nine Grahas

  • Surya (Sun): Authority, vitality, soul purpose, and father figures.
  • Chandra (Moon): Mind, emotions, mother, and inner peace.
  • Mars (Mangal): Energy, courage, property, and sibling bonds.
  • Budh (Mercury): Intelligence, communication, commerce, and education.
  • Guru (Jupiter): Wisdom, fortune, spirituality, and children.
  • Shukra (Venus): Love, luxury, art, marriage, and material comforts.
  • Shani (Saturn): Discipline, karma, longevity, and career.
  • Rahu: Worldly desires, innovation, and karmic lessons.
  • Ketu: Spiritual liberation, detachment, and past-life karma.

Ritual Process

The Nav Grah puja involves invocation of each graha with specific mantras, offerings of corresponding materials (colours, flowers, grains), and Havan (sacred fire ceremony). The puja typically takes 3–4 hours and is performed during auspicious planetary hours.

Planetary Pacification

Grha Shanti

Grha Shanti refers to specific pacification rituals designed to neutralise the malefic effects of planets as identified in your birth chart. Unlike general pujas, Grha Shanti is a targeted remedy prescribed after careful astrological analysis.

When Is Grha Shanti Needed?

  • When a planet occupies a challenging position in the birth chart
  • During difficult planetary periods (Dasha-Antardasha)
  • For Sade Sati (Saturn's transit over Moon sign) or Dhaiya periods
  • When facing persistent obstacles in health, career, or relationships
  • For Kal Sarp Dosha or other specific chart afflictions
  • During major life transitions to ensure smooth progression

Our Grha Shanti Process

First, we analyse your birth chart to identify the exact planetary affliction. Then, we prescribe the specific remedy which may include a combination of Havan, Abhishek, mantra chanting, gemstone wearing, and donation (Daan) — all customised to your unique chart configuration.

Intensive Rituals

Anushthan

Anushthan represents the most intensive form of Vedic ritual practice. These are multi-day, highly structured ceremonies involving continuous mantra chanting, elaborate Havan, and detailed scriptural protocols. Anushthans are performed for profound spiritual transformation, karmic resolution, and deep healing.

Types of Anushthans

  • Maha Mrityunjay Anushthan: For health, longevity, and overcoming life-threatening situations. Involves 125,000 mantra repetitions.
  • Sudarshan Anushthan: For overcoming enemies, legal troubles, and removing persistent obstacles.
  • Navarna Anushthan: Powerful Durga worship for protection, power, and spiritual elevation.
  • Lakshmi Anushthan: For wealth, prosperity, and business growth.
  • Satyanarayan Anushthan: For family peace, harmony, and overall well-being.
  • Custom Anushthan: Tailored to specific needs as prescribed by astrological analysis.

Anushthan Protocol

Anushthans typically span 1–7 days depending on the type. They involve multiple qualified pandits chanting in unison, strict adherence to dietary and behavioural disciplines (Sankalp), and detailed offerings. Each Anushthan concludes with a Purnahuti (final offering) and Prasad distribution.

Life Ceremonies

Vedic Sanskar

Vedic Sanskars are the sacred rites of passage that mark and sanctify every major stage of human life. These ceremonies, described in the Grihya Sutras, transform ordinary life events into spiritually meaningful milestones.

Sanskars We Perform

  • Jatakarma (Birth Ceremony): Welcoming the newborn with sacred rituals, whispering mantras, and invoking divine blessings for the child's life journey.
  • Namkaran (Naming Ceremony): Formal naming of the child based on astrological calculations and family traditions.
  • Annaprashan (First Food Ceremony): The sacred first feeding of solid food to the child.
  • Mundan (Head Shaving Ceremony): First haircut ceremony symbolising purification and new beginnings.
  • Karnavedha (Ear Piercing): Traditional ear-piercing ceremony for health and spiritual protection.
  • Upanayanam (Sacred Thread Ceremony): Initiation into Vedic learning and spiritual discipline for young boys.
  • Vivah (Wedding Ceremony): Complete Hindu wedding rituals following Indian or Nepali traditions, including Ganesh Puja, Kanyadaan, Saptapadi, and more.
  • Griha Pravesh (Housewarming): Vastu-compliant ceremony for entering a new home.
  • Mrityu Sanskar (Last Rites): Antim Kriya and Shradh ceremonies for departed souls.

Indian & Nepali Traditions

We offer ceremonies following both Indian (North Indian, South Indian, Bengali, Maithili) and Nepali (Khas, Newar, Tharu) traditions. Each ceremony is customised to your family's regional customs while maintaining Vedic authenticity.
