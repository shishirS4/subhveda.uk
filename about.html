Our Mission

Preserving Sacred Wisdom for the Modern World

Subhveda was founded with a deep reverence for the Vedic traditions that have guided humanity for thousands of years. We recognised that while the world modernises rapidly, the timeless wisdom of Jyotish Shastra, sacred pujas, and Vedic sanskars remains profoundly relevant for navigating life's challenges.

Our mission is to bridge the ancient and the contemporary — bringing authentic, scripture-based Vedic services to individuals and families across the globe, regardless of geographical boundaries.

We draw from both Indian and Nepali Vedic traditions, honouring the rich diversity of these sacred practices while maintaining the utmost authenticity in every ritual we perform.

Our Philosophy

Rooted in Tradition, Guided by Devotion

Every ritual we perform is an act of devotion, every consultation a journey into self-understanding.

Spiritual Integrity

We never compromise on the authenticity of rituals. Every ceremony follows established Vedic protocols passed down through generations of learned pandits.

Compassionate Service

We understand that each person comes with unique concerns. Our approach is empathetic, personalised, and focused on your genuine spiritual welfare.

Scholarly Excellence

Our practitioners have undergone rigorous Vedic education, studying classical texts including the Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra, Jataka Parijata, and other foundational works.

Cultural Preservation

We are dedicated to keeping Indian and Nepali Vedic traditions alive, adapting delivery methods for the modern age without altering sacred content.

Our Practitioners

Guided by Experienced Vedic Scholars

Head Pandit & Astrologer

PT. Shambhu Parsad Sharma — with over 30 years of dedicated practice in Vedic astrology and ritual ceremonies, our lead practitioner has performed thousands of pujas and anushthans for clients across India, Nepal, and internationally.

Vedic Scholars Team

Dual Heritage

Indian & Nepali Vedic Traditions

Nepal holds a unique place in the Vedic world as one of the last remaining living centres of Sanskrit-based Vedic tradition. The Nepali Vedic tradition has preserved rituals and chanting methods that trace directly back to ancient times.

At Subhveda, we draw from both the Indian and Nepali Vedic lineages. This dual heritage allows us to offer:

  • North Indian Vedic puja methodologies
  • Nepali Vedic chanting and ritual traditions
  • Pan-Indian Jyotish Shastra interpretations
  • Regional ceremony customs (Maithili, Newari, Kumaoni, etc.)
